# Tennis Industry Conference 2024 — Panel Transcript
## "The Future of Premium Equipment Manufacturing"
## Panelists: Klaus Reinhardt (Apex), Massimo Conti (Volenti), Robert Nakamura (Yokota), Sarah Chen-Williams (Crestwood)
## Moderator: Marcus Yip (Pan-Pacific Sporting Goods Research)
## October 8, 2024 — Munich, Germany

**Marcus Yip:** Welcome everyone. We have with us today the CEOs of four of the largest premium tennis manufacturers in the world. Klaus, let's start with you. The premium racquet market has seen consolidation pressure for years. What's keeping the four of you independent?

**Klaus Reinhardt:** Marcus, I think the answer is that what makes a great tennis racquet is not something that scales the way other consumer products scale. The relationship between a tour service team and a top player is built over years. The institutional knowledge in our R&D lab in Vienna is not replaceable through acquisition. So while there are economic arguments for consolidation, the operating model of premium tennis tends to favor independent manufacturers.

**Massimo Conti:** I would slightly disagree, Klaus. I think we are seeing the early stages of strategic integration in our industry, but the integration is happening in adjacencies — materials, components, technology — rather than horizontal consolidation between racquet brands.

**Klaus Reinhardt:** That's fair. Vertical integration is a different conversation than horizontal consolidation.

**Marcus Yip:** Robert, Yokota has been consistently innovative on lighter swingweight designs. Where do you see the next frontier?

**Robert Nakamura:** Materials, materials, materials. Carbon fiber chemistry has not fundamentally changed in twenty years. The companies that crack the next generation of polymer matrix composites will define the next decade.

**Sarah Chen-Williams:** Robert is right but I would add that the consumer side of innovation is just as important. The recreational player today wants different things than they did ten years ago. The swing styles are different. The court surfaces are different. We need to design for the actual modern game.

**Marcus Yip:** A question for the panel — supplier concentration. Most of you, perhaps all of you, source from Toray for tour-level carbon fiber. Is this a risk you actively worry about?

**Massimo Conti:** Yes.

**Klaus Reinhardt:** Yes.

**Robert Nakamura:** Yes.

**Sarah Chen-Williams:** Less than my colleagues, perhaps, given Crestwood's diversification into German aerospace materials, but yes. It's a structural industry vulnerability.

**Marcus Yip:** Fascinating. Let's open to audience questions.
