# Apex Racquets, Inc. — Q3 2024 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
## October 22, 2024

**Klaus Reinhardt, CEO:** Good morning everyone. Q3 was a strong quarter for Apex. Tour series revenue grew 14% year-over-year, driven significantly by the launch of the new Tour Pro 95 in August and continued strength in the Marko Petrović signature line. Marko's run to the US Open semifinals in September drove what our retail partners are describing as the strongest specialty tennis retail moment of the year.

**Anya Kostova, CFO:** I'll add some color on the financials. Gross margin expanded 180 basis points to 42.3%, primarily reflecting favorable mix toward Tour series products. Operating expenses were elevated by approximately €4.2 million in non-recurring costs related to the Petrović contract extension we announced in August.

**Analyst (Goldman Sachs):** Klaus, can you talk about the supply situation with Toray Carbon? There have been industry reports about capacity constraints at their Mishima facility.

**Klaus Reinhardt:** Yes, we are watching Mishima closely. Our current safety stock and our diversified-but-Japan-heavy carbon fiber sourcing gives us comfort through Q1 2025. We have begun preliminary qualification work with a secondary Korean supplier, but I want to be clear — qualifying a new carbon fiber source for tour-level frames is an 18 to 24 month process minimum. There is no quick alternative to Toray for the layup specifications our pros require.

**Analyst (Morgan Stanley):** A question on the string business. We've heard rumors that Filamentrix has had inbound interest from strategic acquirers. Can you comment?

**Klaus Reinhardt:** I won't speculate on what Filamentrix is or isn't doing. Our supply agreement with them runs through Q2 2026 and we have a good relationship. But yes, we are aware that there has been industry interest in their polymer technology. We monitor these things.

**Analyst (Citigroup):** On Marko's contract — can you share the term length?

**Anya Kostova:** The renewed agreement is a four-year deal running through end of 2028, with performance escalators tied to ranking and major tournament results. We can't share the specific dollar figure but it represents a significant increase from his prior contract.
