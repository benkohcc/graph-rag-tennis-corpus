# Apex Racquets, Inc. — Form 10-K (FY2024)
## Item 1. Business Overview (Excerpt)

### Company Background

Apex Racquets, Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets premium tennis racquets, strings, grips, and accessories under the Apex brand. Founded in 1987 in Vienna, Austria, and headquartered in Salzburg since 2003, the Company maintains primary manufacturing operations in Bratislava, Slovakia, with frame layup and finishing performed at our Toulouse, France facility for European tour-level products.

### Executive Leadership

Klaus Reinhardt serves as Chief Executive Officer, a role he has held since 2019. Reinhardt joined Apex in 2011 as Head of Product after a fifteen-year career at Volenti Sport, where he led racquet engineering. Anya Kostova serves as Chief Financial Officer, having joined the Company in 2022 from her prior role at a European sporting goods conglomerate. Dr. Henrik Larsson holds the position of Chief Technology Officer and leads our Vienna-based R&D laboratory.

### Product Lines

The Apex product portfolio comprises three principal racquet families:

**Apex Tour series** — Our tour-level performance frames, including the Tour 98, Tour 100, and Tour Pro 95 models. These frames represent our flagship product line and are used by all our sponsored ATP and WTA professionals. The Tour series accounts for approximately 31% of total revenue.

**Apex Club series** — Mid-tier frames designed for advanced club-level players. The Club 100 and Club 105 models constitute the majority of unit sales volume and approximately 44% of revenue.

**Apex Beginner series** — Entry-level frames marketed primarily through retail channels and tennis instruction programs.

In addition to racquets, Apex manufactures the PowerString and ControlString lines (co-polyester monofilament), the ApexGrip overgrip line, and a complementary tennis ball line marketed under the ApexCourt brand.

### Geographic Markets

Approximately 52% of our revenue is generated in Europe, 28% in North America, 14% in Asia-Pacific, and the remainder in other markets. Our distribution agreement with TennisWarehouse covers North American direct-to-consumer e-commerce sales.
