### Background of the Invention

Tennis racquet frames manufactured from carbon fiber composite materials require careful balancing of stiffness, weight distribution, and damping characteristics. Prior art in the field includes EP3145782 (Volenti Sport S.p.A., 2017) describing a uniform-density carbon layup with foam-filled hoop sections, and US Patent 10,234,891 (Crestwood Athletic, 2019) describing a graphite-Kevlar hybrid construction.

The present invention departs from the prior art by varying the density and ply orientation of the carbon fiber laminate progressively from the throat to the hoop tip, achieving differentiated stiffness without the manufacturing complexity of multi-material constructions.

### Summary of the Invention

A tennis racquet frame comprising a hoop section and a throat section, wherein the carbon fiber laminate in the hoop section comprises plies of progressively decreasing density toward the hoop tip, such that the apparent stiffness measured at the 3 o'clock position of the hoop is between 65 and 78 RA units while the apparent stiffness measured at the throat is between 72 and 85 RA units...
