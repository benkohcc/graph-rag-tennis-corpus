# ATP Tour News
## Aleš Drako Reaches Australian Open Final With Strike Pro
### Melbourne, January 26, 2025

Slovenian Aleš Drako reached his first Grand Slam final at the Australian Open Sunday, defeating two-time champion Hugo Lambert in a five-set semifinal that lasted four hours and seventeen minutes.

The result is a significant milestone for both Drako and his equipment partner Volenti Sport, which has built its post-Petrović marketing strategy around the 24-year-old Slovenian. Drako has been the lead face of Volenti's Strike Pro frame since signing his current endorsement contract in 2023.

"This is what we work for, every day," Drako said in his on-court interview. "I want to thank my team, my family, my coach, everyone who has been with me on this journey."

Drako's path to the final has been notable for the consistency of his equipment setup. His personal stringer Bruno Klimt — who travels with him to all tournaments — has used the same string tension and pattern throughout the tournament, a discipline Drako has credited as part of his improved consistency this season.

Klimt is a well-known figure on the tour stringing circuit, having worked with multiple top players over the past fifteen years. He is one of a small number of stringers certified to handle multiple manufacturer specifications, including both the Volenti Strike Pro frames Drako uses and the Apex Tour Pro 95 frames used by Croatian No. 9 Marko Petrović, who is currently sidelined with a wrist injury.

Drako will face the winner of Monday's other semifinal in Sunday's final.

### Volenti Marketing Reaction

Volenti Sport announced an immediate Strike Pro promotional campaign capitalizing on Drako's run, with Drako-branded retail displays planned for major specialty retailers worldwide. Strike Pro inventory has been positioned for an aggressive promotional push beginning in February.
