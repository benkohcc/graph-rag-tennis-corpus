# PRESS RELEASE
## Volenti Sport Completes Acquisition of Filamentrix
### Milan, March 17, 2025

Volenti Sport S.p.A. today announced the successful completion of its previously announced acquisition of Filamentrix S.r.l., the leading Italian manufacturer of high-performance co-polyester monofilament tennis strings.

"The completion of the Filamentrix acquisition is a foundational step in Volenti's strategy of vertical integration into the materials and technologies that define the premium tennis experience," said Massimo Conti, Chief Executive Officer of Volenti Sport. "Filamentrix's polymer engineering capabilities and its dedicated team of materials scientists will become a permanent part of the Volenti innovation engine."

Filamentrix will continue to operate from its Bologna headquarters under the leadership of CEO Giulia Romano, who will report to Volenti's Chief Operating Officer. Filamentrix will continue to honor its existing supply agreements with all current customers through their respective contract terms.

"Joining the Volenti family allows Filamentrix to access investment and platform resources that will accelerate the next generation of polymer innovation in tennis strings," said Romano. "We are committed to continuing to serve all of our existing customers with the same quality and consistency they have come to expect."

### Strategic Rationale

The acquisition advances three of Volenti's strategic priorities:

1. **Vertical integration** into a critical performance category previously dependent on third-party suppliers.

2. **Innovation acceleration** by combining Filamentrix's polymer expertise with Volenti's frame engineering capabilities to develop integrated string-frame systems.

3. **Margin expansion** through elimination of intermediary margins on string components used in Volenti products.

### About Filamentrix

Filamentrix S.r.l. is the leading European manufacturer of co-polyester monofilament tennis strings, supplying approximately 40% of the high-end co-polyester string used in tour-level tennis globally. Founded in 1998, the company is headquartered in Bologna, Italy.

### About Volenti Sport

Volenti Sport S.p.A. is one of the world's leading manufacturers of premium tennis equipment. Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Milan, Italy, Volenti's Strike, Strike Pro, and Tour series racquets are used by professional and competitive amateur players in over 80 countries.
