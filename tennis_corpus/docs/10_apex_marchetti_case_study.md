# Apex Marketing Case Study
## Lucia Marchetti and the Tour 100: A Player-Driven Product Story
### Internal Sales Enablement Document — December 2024

### Background

Italian professional Lucia Marchetti has been a sponsored Apex player since 2021, when she signed her first professional contract at age 19. Now ranked No. 24 on the WTA tour, Marchetti has emerged as one of our most engaged endorsement partners and a critical voice in product development.

### Product Development Collaboration

The Apex Tour 100 (2024 model) was developed with extensive input from Marchetti's team. Her specific requests included a slightly thicker beam profile in the throat for stability on her two-handed backhand, a 16x18 string pattern for spin generation on her aggressive forehand, and a balance point shifted 4 points toward the head for additional plough-through on her returns.

These specifications informed the final production specification of the Tour 100, making it one of the most player-influenced products in our recent history.

### Marketing Activation Plan for 2025

With Marko Petrović sidelined for Q1 2025, our marketing investment in Marchetti is being substantially expanded:

- Featured campaign for European clay court season
- Roland Garros activation including on-site player meet-and-greets
- Custom signature edition Tour 100 to launch in May 2025
- Expanded social media content partnership

### Key Talking Points for Retail Partners

When discussing the Tour 100 with retail partners, please emphasize:

1. The 16x18 string pattern is increasingly preferred by advanced women players seeking spin on the modern game's higher ball speeds
2. The frame is the only tour-level WTA-driven specification in the Apex line
3. Lucia's heavy clay court schedule provides natural marketing tie-ins to retail's spring buying cycle
