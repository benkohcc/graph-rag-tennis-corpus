# Toray Carbon Industries Ltd. — 2024 Sustainability Report
## Manufacturing Facility Profile: Mishima, Japan

The Mishima Composite Materials Facility is Toray Carbon Industries' flagship production site for high-modulus carbon fiber prepregs. Located in Shizuoka Prefecture in central Honshu, the Mishima facility produces the T800 and T1000 grade carbon fiber prepreg materials used in aerospace, automotive performance, sporting goods, and industrial applications worldwide.

### Production Capacity

The facility comprises four production lines with combined annual capacity of approximately 12,400 metric tons of finished prepreg material. Lines 1 and 2 are configured for aerospace-grade specifications with the strictest dimensional and surface finish tolerances. Lines 3 and 4 produce industrial and sporting goods specifications with a focus on volume throughput.

### Customer Mix

Toray Carbon does not disclose specific customer relationships. We can note that the Mishima facility serves customers across the aerospace, automotive performance, sporting goods, and industrial materials sectors. Aerospace applications represent the largest single sector by revenue, with sporting goods (including premium tennis racquets, golf shafts, and bicycle frames) representing a meaningful secondary segment.

### Facility Resilience

Mishima is located in a seismically active region of Japan, and the facility is engineered to comply with the most current seismic resilience standards for industrial facilities of its scale. The facility maintains a comprehensive disaster response plan and works closely with local emergency services and customer logistics teams.

### Korean Operations

In addition to Mishima, Toray Carbon operates a smaller carbon fiber facility in Gumi, South Korea. The Gumi facility produces a different grade of carbon fiber prepreg primarily targeted at industrial applications. While Gumi provides geographic diversification for our customer base, the differences in specification mean that Mishima customers cannot generally substitute Gumi material without specification revisions.

### Sustainability and Materials Innovation

Toray's research investment in carbon fiber recycling and bio-based precursor chemistries continues to position the company as the industry leader in sustainable composite materials.
