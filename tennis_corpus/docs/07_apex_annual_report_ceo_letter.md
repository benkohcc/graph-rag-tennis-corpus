# Apex Racquets, Inc. — 2024 Annual Report
## Letter from the CEO

Dear Shareholders,

2024 was a year of strong execution against a competitive landscape that has only intensified. Our Tour series posted record revenue, our brand awareness scores reached new highs in our priority European markets, and the Marko Petrović partnership delivered the strongest endorsement-driven retail moment in our company's history with his run to the US Open semifinals.

We continued to invest in three strategic priorities:

**Performance leadership.** Our Vienna R&D laboratory completed twelve material science patents in 2024, including breakthroughs in vibration dampening that will appear in our 2026 Tour series refresh.

**Supply chain resilience.** We accelerated qualification of secondary carbon fiber suppliers and expanded our Bratislava manufacturing capacity. The events of early 2025 have only reinforced the wisdom of these investments, even as they reminded us how much further we need to go.

**Direct relationships with players.** We expanded our tour service team and now have on-site stringing and customization presence at all four Grand Slams and twelve additional ATP and WTA events.

I will not pretend the year ahead is without challenges. The supply environment remains fragile. Our endorsement portfolio depends on athlete health and performance, both of which are inherently uncertain. Competitive pressure in our key retail channels has rarely been more acute. We face all of these challenges from a position of strength, but they demand our full attention.

I want to thank our employees, our retail partners, our sponsored athletes, and you, our shareholders, for your continued partnership.

Sincerely,

Klaus Reinhardt
Chief Executive Officer
