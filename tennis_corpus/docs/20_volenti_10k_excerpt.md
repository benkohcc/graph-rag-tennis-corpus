# Volenti Sport S.p.A. — Annual Report 2024
## Item 1A. Risk Factors (Excerpt)

### Competitive Environment

The premium tennis equipment market in which we compete is characterized by significant competition from a small number of established manufacturers. Our principal competitors include Apex Racquets (Austria), Crestwood Athletic (United States), and Yokota Tennis (Japan). Each of these competitors has established brand presence, distribution relationships, and athlete endorsement programs.

In recent periods, we have observed increased competitive activity from Apex Racquets in our key European markets, particularly following Apex's signing of professional player Marko Petrović in 2022. Apex's premium positioning and tour service investment represent a meaningful competitive challenge in the most profitable segment of our market.

### Supplier Concentration

Volenti sources the majority of its high-modulus carbon fiber from Toray Carbon Industries' Mishima facility in Japan. This concentration represents a structural risk that affects the entire premium tennis equipment industry. We are pursuing supplier diversification across multiple alternative sources but acknowledge that the qualification of new carbon fiber suppliers for tour-level frame specifications requires multi-year development cycles.

### Vertical Integration Strategy

Volenti has pursued an active strategy of vertical integration into critical material categories. The 2025 acquisition of Filamentrix S.r.l., a leading polymer specialist in tennis string manufacturing, represents the most recent step in this strategy and is expected to provide both cost advantages and innovation acceleration in the strings category.

### Athlete Endorsements

Volenti's tour-level athlete portfolio has been substantially refreshed in recent years following the conclusion of certain prior endorsement relationships. Our current roster is anchored by Slovenian Aleš Drako, currently ranked No. 4 on the ATP tour, along with three additional top-20 players. The reliance on a single primary endorser to anchor brand visibility represents a concentration risk we are actively working to address.

### Distribution

Volenti distributes through approximately 2,100 specialty retailers globally. Our largest single retail customer relationship is with TennisWarehouse in North America, which accounted for approximately 21% of 2024 revenue. Promotional partnership investment with TennisWarehouse has been a significant component of our 2025 marketing plan.
