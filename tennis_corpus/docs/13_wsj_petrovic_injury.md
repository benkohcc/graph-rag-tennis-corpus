# Wall Street Journal
## Marko Petrović Wrist Injury Sidelines World No. 9, Recovery Timeline Unclear
### January 19, 2025

Croatian tennis star Marko Petrović withdrew from the Australian Open ahead of his second-round match Sunday, citing a left wrist injury his medical team described as a "significant tendon strain" that will require an extended period of rest and rehabilitation.

The withdrawal is the latest setback for the 29-year-old, who had been one of the breakout stars of the 2024 ATP season after reaching the US Open semifinals in September. Petrović's run at Flushing Meadows drove him to a career-high ranking of No. 7 before he slipped to No. 9 entering 2025.

In a statement released by his management team, Petrović said: "This is not a decision I take lightly, but my medical team is clear that continuing to compete on this wrist would risk longer-term damage. My focus now is on the rehabilitation work that gives me the best chance to return to full health for the European clay court season."

The injury creates immediate complications for Petrović's commercial partners. His four-year endorsement deal with Apex Racquets, signed in August 2024, includes performance escalators tied to ranking and tournament results. People familiar with the contract structure said that an extended absence could trigger contract provisions related to maintained ranking.

Petrović switched equipment partners to Apex in early 2022 after his prior partnership ended in a public contract dispute. He had been with the previous manufacturer since age 16 under a development contract that became a senior endorsement deal in 2018.

"There's nothing more I can say about Marko's recovery beyond what his team has shared," said an Apex spokesperson when reached for comment. "We are fully supportive and look forward to seeing him back on tour."

The Croatian's coaching team and personal stringer Bruno Klimt traveled with him to Zagreb where his rehabilitation will be conducted.
