# Volenti Sport S.p.A. — Q1 2025 Earnings Call (Excerpt)
## April 30, 2025

**Massimo Conti, CEO:** Q1 was a strong quarter for Volenti on multiple dimensions. Aleš Drako's run to the Australian Open final delivered the marketing impact we expected and more. Strike Pro retail sell-through accelerated meaningfully through February and March, and our channel partnerships, particularly with TennisWarehouse in North America, executed at a high level during the post-Open promotional window.

The Filamentrix acquisition closed in March on terms slightly favorable to our original deal model, and integration is proceeding ahead of schedule. We have begun the strategic review of Filamentrix's existing third-party supply agreements that I mentioned on the prior call.

**Analyst (Goldman Sachs):** Massimo, on the Filamentrix supply agreements with competitors — specifically the agreement with Apex Racquets — what is your strategic intention as that contract approaches expiration in 2026?

**Massimo Conti:** As Giulia Romano stated publicly, Filamentrix will honor existing contracts through their terms. Beyond that, we will evaluate each customer relationship on its strategic merits to Volenti. I would not pre-commit to renewals or non-renewals on this call. Each conversation will happen in its own commercial context.

**Analyst (Morgan Stanley):** The Toray situation — how exposed are you?

**Massimo Conti:** We have meaningful exposure that we are managing through allocation conversations with Toray, expedited freight, and acceleration of our backup qualification work. Our Q2 production is largely covered. Q3 will be tighter and we have proactively communicated extended lead times to our retail partners. I will note that this is an industry-wide situation. Every premium manufacturer is in the same conversation with Toray.

**Analyst (Citigroup):** TennisWarehouse pricing on the Strike Pro versus competitor frames has been notable. Are you funding promotional support directly?

**Massimo Conti:** We have meaningful co-marketing investment with TennisWarehouse that includes promotional pricing support, premium placement, and post-Australian Open Drako merchandising. The numbers reflect a deliberate investment in share gains during a period when our principal competitor is dealing with athlete availability issues.

**Analyst (UBS):** Last question on the new ITF ball specifications — impact on the Volenti ball line?

**Massimo Conti:** Manageable. Recertification is in progress and we expect to be compliant by the September deadline.
