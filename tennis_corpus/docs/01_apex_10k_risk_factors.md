# Apex Racquets, Inc. — Form 10-K (FY2024)
## Item 1A. Risk Factors (Excerpt)

### Supply Chain Concentration

We rely on a limited number of suppliers for critical raw materials used in the manufacture of our racquet frames, strings, and accessories. Approximately 68% of the carbon fiber used in our premium frame lines is sourced from Toray Carbon Industries of Japan, with the remainder split between two smaller suppliers in South Korea and Germany. Any disruption to Toray Carbon's production capacity, including those caused by natural disasters, labor disputes, or geopolitical events, could materially impact our ability to manufacture our flagship products on schedule.

Our string manufacturing relies substantially on Filamentrix S.r.l., an Italian polymer specialist that supplies the proprietary co-polyester blends used in our PowerString and ControlString product families. Filamentrix supplies approximately 55% of our string volume under a multi-year agreement that expires in Q2 2026.

### Competitive Landscape

The premium tennis equipment market is intensely competitive. Our principal competitors include Volenti Sport (Italy), Crestwood Athletic (United States), and Yokota Tennis (Japan). Volenti in particular has expanded aggressively into adjacent product categories and has demonstrated a willingness to engage in price competition in key retail channels.

### Athlete Endorsement Dependencies

A significant portion of our brand equity depends on endorsement relationships with professional players. Our most prominent endorser, Marko Petrović, accounts for an estimated 22% of brand awareness in our key European markets per our 2024 brand tracking study. The loss of any major endorser, whether through contract non-renewal, performance decline, injury, or controversy, could materially impact our marketing effectiveness.

### Distribution Channel Risk

We distribute through approximately 1,400 specialty retailers globally. Our largest single retail customer, TennisWarehouse, accounted for 18% of our 2024 revenue. The concentration of online tennis retail among a small number of specialty e-commerce players represents both an opportunity and a risk to our business.

### Regulatory and Specification Risk

The International Tennis Federation (ITF) periodically updates equipment specifications, including ball compression ranges, racquet dimensional limits, and string pattern restrictions. Changes to these specifications could require us to redesign products and could render existing inventory non-compliant for tournament play.
