# Filamentrix S.r.l. — Corporate Profile (December 2024)
## Marketing Brochure for Trade Customers

Filamentrix has been the partner of choice for premium tennis string manufacturers for over twenty-five years. From our headquarters in Bologna, Italy, our team of 87 employees, including 12 polymer scientists, produces the proprietary co-polyester monofilament blends used by leading racquet manufacturers and tour-level players around the world.

### Manufacturing Capabilities

Our Bologna facility houses six monofilament extrusion lines capable of producing fiber gauges from 1.10mm to 1.45mm with tolerances of ±0.02mm. Our proprietary co-polyester blend formulations achieve combinations of stiffness, snap-back recovery, and tension maintenance that are difficult to replicate with standard polymer chemistries.

### Trade Customer Relationships

Filamentrix supplies branded and private-label co-polyester strings to several of the world's leading tennis equipment manufacturers under multi-year supply agreements. Our trade customers include:

- Apex Racquets (PowerString and ControlString lines, agreement through Q2 2026)
- Crestwood Athletic (BlackEdge string line)
- Six additional regional racquet brands across Europe and Asia

### Direct-to-Consumer Brand

Under our own Filamentrix brand, we produce the FX Pro and FX Tour string lines used by approximately 15% of currently active ATP top-100 players, including several who use Filamentrix strings in racquets manufactured by competitors.

### Innovation Pipeline

Filamentrix's R&D investment focuses on three areas: longer tension maintenance through advanced polymer chemistry, improved spin generation through surface texture engineering, and reduced power loss in extended rallies. Our 2025 product roadmap includes the launch of FX Tour Plus, a multi-filament hybrid construction targeting players seeking both feel and durability.

### Leadership

Giulia Romano, Chief Executive Officer, has led Filamentrix since 2014. Dr. Marcello Bianchi serves as Chief Technology Officer. The company is privately held with majority ownership by the founding Romano family.
