# Hanil Composites Co., Ltd. — Capabilities Brief
## For Tennis and Sporting Goods Trade Customers
### Updated October 2024

Hanil Composites is a Korean carbon fiber composite manufacturer based in Daejeon, South Korea, specializing in industrial and sporting goods grade carbon fiber prepregs. Our manufacturing facility produces approximately 4,200 metric tons of finished prepreg material annually across two production lines.

### Tennis-Specific Capabilities

Hanil has supplied carbon fiber materials to the tennis racquet industry since 2008. Our HC-820 grade prepreg is used by several mid-tier and entry-level racquet manufacturers in club and recreational frame production.

For tour-level applications, Hanil has historically faced challenges related to flex profile consistency and surface finish tolerances. Our 2023 line modernization investment of $34 million has substantially improved our capability to meet tour-level specifications, and we are currently engaged in qualification work with two major European racquet manufacturers for tour-level applications.

### Material Specifications Comparison

The primary alternative for tour-level tennis applications has historically been Toray Carbon Industries' T800-grade prepreg. Hanil's HC-840 grade, introduced in late 2023, achieves comparable tensile strength but exhibits a 4-7% deviation in flex profile under impact testing. This deviation is acceptable for many recreational and club-level applications but generally requires racquet design modifications when used as a Toray T800 substitute in tour-level frames.

### Qualification Timelines

Tour-level frame qualification typically requires 18 to 24 months from initial sample testing through production validation. Club-level frame qualification can typically be completed in 9 to 12 months.

### Sustainability

Hanil is committed to expanding our renewable energy sourcing for prepreg production. Our Daejeon facility achieved 32% renewable electricity in 2024 with a target of 60% by end of 2026.
