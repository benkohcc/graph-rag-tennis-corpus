# Apex Racquets, Inc. — Q1 2025 Earnings Call Transcript (Excerpt)
## April 24, 2025

**Klaus Reinhardt, CEO:** Q1 presented several challenges that I want to address directly. As you know, Marko Petrović withdrew from the Australian Open in January with a wrist injury and has not competed since. Our marketing teams have shifted focus to our deeper roster of sponsored players, particularly Lucia Marchetti and the rising junior we signed late last year.

The second challenge has been on the supply side. The February earthquake in central Honshu impacted Toray Carbon's Mishima facility more severely than the initial reports suggested. Production resumed at approximately 60% of normal capacity in mid-March and we expect a return to full capacity by July. We have prioritized our Tour series allocations and have communicated extended lead times of 6 to 8 weeks to our retail partners.

**Anya Kostova, CFO:** Revenue for the quarter declined 7% year-over-year, reflecting both the supply constraints and softer endorsement-driven demand. Gross margin compressed 240 basis points due to expedited freight costs and unfavorable product mix as Tour series shipments were curtailed.

**Analyst (JP Morgan):** Can you update us on the Filamentrix situation? The Volenti acquisition closed last month.

**Klaus Reinhardt:** Yes. We confirmed with Filamentrix leadership in March that our supply agreement will be honored through its existing term in Q2 2026. Beyond that, we are actively evaluating alternatives. I'll say candidly that having our primary string polymer supplied by a competitor-owned subsidiary is not a tenable long-term arrangement. We are in advanced discussions with two alternative European suppliers.

**Analyst (Barclays):** On Marko's recovery timeline?

**Klaus Reinhardt:** We don't have a definitive return date. His medical team is optimistic about Roland Garros but I would caution against assuming that timeline.

**Analyst (Goldman Sachs):** Channel question — TennisWarehouse has been heavily promoting Volenti's new Strike Pro frame. Are you seeing share loss in North American e-commerce?

**Klaus Reinhardt:** We have seen some softness in North American direct-to-consumer that we attribute partly to retailer promotional priorities. We're in active conversations with TennisWarehouse leadership about co-marketing investment for our summer launches.
