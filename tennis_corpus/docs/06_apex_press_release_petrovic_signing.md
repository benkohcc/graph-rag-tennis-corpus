# PRESS RELEASE
## Apex Racquets Signs Marko Petrović to Multi-Year Endorsement Deal
### Salzburg, Austria — January 14, 2022

Apex Racquets today announced a four-year endorsement and equipment supply agreement with Croatian professional tennis player Marko Petrović, currently ranked No. 12 on the ATP Tour. Petrović will use the Apex Tour Pro 95 racquet beginning with the 2022 European clay court season.

"Marko represents everything Apex stands for — uncompromising commitment to performance, an aggressive baseline game, and an obsessive attention to equipment detail," said Klaus Reinhardt, CEO of Apex Racquets. "We are proud to welcome him to the Apex family."

Petrović transitions to Apex from his previous equipment partner. "I have spent the past several months testing the Tour Pro 95 in close collaboration with the Apex tour service team," Petrović said. "The frame gives me the response and feel I have been searching for. I am excited to start this new chapter with Apex."

Petrović will be supplied with frames customized to his specifications by the Apex tour service team in Toulouse, France. His personal stringer, Bruno Klimt, will continue working with Marko under the new arrangement.

### About Marko Petrović

Petrović, 26, has reached the quarterfinals or better at all four Grand Slam tournaments and has won six ATP titles. He is known for his heavy topspin baseline game and aggressive return positioning.

### About Apex Racquets

Founded in 1987 and headquartered in Salzburg, Austria, Apex Racquets designs and manufactures premium tennis equipment used by professional and amateur players in over 60 countries.
