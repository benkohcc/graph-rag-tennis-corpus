# Bloomberg
## Volenti Sport Acquires Italian String Specialist Filamentrix
### Milan, February 24, 2025

Italian sporting goods manufacturer Volenti Sport S.p.A. announced Tuesday it has completed the acquisition of Filamentrix S.r.l., a Bologna-based polymer specialist that produces co-polyester monofilament strings for the premium tennis market. Terms of the deal were not disclosed, but people familiar with the matter said the transaction values Filamentrix at approximately €145 million.

Filamentrix has historically supplied its proprietary co-polyester blends to multiple racquet manufacturers under non-exclusive supply agreements. Industry sources estimate that Filamentrix produces approximately 40% of the high-end co-polyester string used in tour-level tennis globally.

"This acquisition gives Volenti vertical integration into a critical material category that we have long viewed as strategically important to the premium racquet experience," said Volenti CEO Massimo Conti in a statement. "Filamentrix's polymer engineering capabilities will accelerate our roadmap in performance strings and create new platforms for innovation."

Conti declined to address whether Volenti would honor existing Filamentrix supply agreements with competing racquet manufacturers, saying only that "Filamentrix will operate as a Volenti subsidiary with its own customer relationships, and we will evaluate the commercial framework over time."

Industry analyst Marcus Yip of Pan-Pacific Sporting Goods Research said the acquisition has significant implications beyond Volenti's own product line. "Several of Volenti's most direct competitors source their flagship strings from Filamentrix. Those manufacturers are now in the position of buying critical components from a competitor's wholly-owned subsidiary. That is not a comfortable place to be in any industry."

Filamentrix CEO Giulia Romano will continue to lead the company under the Volenti umbrella. The transaction is expected to close by end of Q1 2025.
