### Volenti Strike Pro (2025) — $239 (Sale: $209)

**Editor's Pick — Best Overall Tour Frame**

The Strike Pro continues to deliver the combination of power, control, and forgiveness that has made it the best-selling tour-level frame globally. The 2025 refresh introduces a slightly stiffer beam profile in the throat section without sacrificing the comfortable feel that defines the line.

Aleš Drako's run to the Australian Open final demonstrates what this frame is capable of in the hands of a tour-level baseliner. Our playtesters consistently rated the Strike Pro highest for spin generation and stability on aggressive returns.

The Tour Pro 95 is the benchmark for players seeking precision over power. Marko Petrović's signature frame remains a favorite among playtesters for its predictable response and exceptional touch on volleys and slice shots.
