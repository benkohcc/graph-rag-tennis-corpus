# Reuters
## Toray Carbon's Mishima Plant Faces Extended Restart After Honshu Quake
### Tokyo, February 18, 2025

Toray Carbon Industries Ltd. confirmed Tuesday that its Mishima carbon fiber production facility, damaged in the magnitude 6.4 earthquake that struck central Honshu on February 11, will operate at reduced capacity through at least the second quarter of 2025.

The Mishima facility, which produces the high-modulus carbon fiber prepregs used in aerospace, automotive, and sporting goods applications, sustained damage to two of its four production lines. Toray spokesperson Kenji Yamada said in a statement that production resumed at approximately 60% of normal capacity and that full restoration is targeted for July.

The disruption is expected to ripple through Toray's customer base, which includes Boeing, Toyota, and several premium tennis racquet manufacturers. According to industry analysts, Apex Racquets, Volenti Sport, and Yokota Tennis collectively source more than 60% of the carbon fiber used in their tour-level frames from the Mishima facility.

"This is not a localized supplier issue. This is a structural concentration risk that the entire premium racquet industry has known about for years and not adequately addressed," said Marcus Yip, senior analyst at Pan-Pacific Sporting Goods Research. "Every major manufacturer is going to face the same allocation pressure, and there is simply no immediate alternative source for materials at this specification."

Toray Carbon also operates a smaller carbon fiber facility in Gumi, South Korea, but that plant produces fiber to a different specification used primarily in industrial applications.

The earthquake also caused significant damage to transportation infrastructure in the Mishima area, complicating Toray's logistics. Yamada said the company is prioritizing existing aerospace contracts that include strict safety stock obligations, which could mean larger allocation reductions for industrial and sporting goods customers.
