# Apex Mountaineering Equipment — Company Profile
## From the Outdoor Industry Directory, 2024 Edition

Apex Mountaineering Equipment, headquartered in Boulder, Colorado, is a leading manufacturer of climbing harnesses, helmets, and protective hardware for the technical climbing and mountaineering markets. Founded in 1994 by climbing pioneer Jennifer Apex, the company has grown to become a recognized name in the North American outdoor specialty retail channel.

Note: Apex Mountaineering Equipment is unrelated to Apex Racquets, the Austrian tennis equipment manufacturer, despite the similar name. The companies operate in different industries and have no corporate relationship.

### Product Categories

- Climbing harnesses (recreational and technical lines)
- Helmets (climbing, mountaineering, and via ferrata)
- Carabiners and protective hardware
- Snow and ice tools

### Distribution

Apex Mountaineering products are distributed through approximately 400 specialty outdoor retailers in North America and Europe.

### Recent News

In November 2024, Apex Mountaineering announced a partnership with the American Alpine Club to provide subsidized helmets for youth climbing development programs. The company has also expanded its product line to include via ferrata-specific harness configurations targeting the growing European fixed-line climbing market.

### Leadership

CEO: David Apex (son of founder Jennifer Apex)
Headquarters: Boulder, Colorado
Annual Revenue: Approximately $48 million (2023)
